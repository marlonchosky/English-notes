# Secondary Predicates - Depictives and Resultatives

### 1. He baked the bread brown
- He: Subject and Pronoun
- baked: CTV
- the bread: DO and NP
- brown: Resultative Object Complement

### 2. I saw the room clean
- I: Subject and Pronoun
- saw: CTV
- the room: DO and NP
- clean: Depictive Adjective and OC

### 3. He died a hero
- He: Subject and Pronoun
- died: Unaccusative Verb
- a hero: Subject-Oriented Depictive Adjunct and NP
