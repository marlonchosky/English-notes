# Verbal Complements and Transitivity

### 1. He bought his brother a car
- He: Subject and Pronoun
- bought: Ditransitive Verb
- his brother: IO and NP
  - his: Determiner
  - brother: Head word
- a car: DO and NP
  - a: Determiner
  - car: Head word

### 2. A number of miners are waiting
- A number of miners: Subject and NP
  - A number of: Complex Phrasal Quantifier
  - miners: Head word
- are waiting: Verb Phrase
  - are: Auxiliary verb
  - waiting: Head word and Intransitive

### 3. The food turned bad
- The food: Subject and NP
- turned: Linking Verb
- bad: Subject Complement and Adjective

### 4. The tired workers in the office are drinking water
- The tired workers in the office: Subject and NP
  - The: Central determiner
  - tired: Attributive Adjective
  - workers: Head word
  - in the office: Adjectival Post-Modifier and PP
- are drinking: Verb Phrase
  - are: Auxiliary Verb
  - drinking: Head word
- water: DO of drink

### 5. I protected the house from fire
- I: Subject and Pronoun
- protected: Prepositional Transitive Verb
- the house: DO and NP
- from fire: PP and Prepositional Complement

### 6. I left him with the bill
- I: Subject and Pronoun
- left: CTV
- him: DO
- with the bill: Prepositional Object Complement, PP

### 7. The milk went sour
- The milk: Subject and NP
- went: Linking Verb
- sour: Subject Complement and Adjective

### 8. I left the keys on the counter
- I: Subject and Pronoun
- left: CTV
- the keys: DO and NP
- on the counter: PP and Locative Adverbial Complement
