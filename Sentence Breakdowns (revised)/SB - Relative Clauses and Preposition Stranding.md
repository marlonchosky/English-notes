# Relative Clauses and Preposition Stranding

### 1. I have something that I can sit next to
- I: Subject and Pronoun
- have: Monotransitive Verb
- something that I can sit next to: NP and DO of have
  - something: Head word and compound indefinite pronoun
  - that I can sit next to: Adjectival Post-Modifier and Relative Clause
    - that: Relative Pronoun / Relativizer (co-indexed with something)
    - I: Subject of the clause
    - can sit: Verb Phrase
      - can: Modal Verb
      - sit: Intransitive Verb
    - next to: Stranded Complex Preposition
