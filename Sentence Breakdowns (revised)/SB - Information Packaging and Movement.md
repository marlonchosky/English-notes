# Information Packaging and Movement

### 1. Play the game out
- Sentence Type: Imperative Clause (Command/Instruction)
- Subject: Covert / Null Subject (You)
- Play out: Transitive Phrasal Verb
  - Play: Lexical Verb Head
  - out: Adverbial Particle / Intransitive Preposition (P°) acting as an aspectual completion marker (meaning to play until conclusion)
- the game: DO and NP

### 2. Here it is.
- Here: Adverb of Place. Fronted Locative Complement
- it: Pronoun and Subject
- is: Linking Verb
- Canonical Declarative Order: It is here
