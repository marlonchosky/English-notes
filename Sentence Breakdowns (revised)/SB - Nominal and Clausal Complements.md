# Nominal and Clausal Complements

### 1. The destruction of the city was horrible
- The destruction of the city: Subject and NP
  - The: Central determiner
  - destruction: Head word
  - of the city: Noun Complement and PP
- was: Linking Verb
- horrible: Subject Complement and Adjective

### 2. The last dog of my parents' pets is nice
- The last dog of my parents' pets: Subject and NP
  - The: Central determiner
  - last: Post-Determiner Ordinal
  - dog: Head word
  - of my parents' pets: Adjectival Post-Modifier and PP
    - of: Head word
    - my parents' pets: NP and Object of the Preposition
      - my parents': Genitive DP Specifier
        - my: Central Determiner
        - parents: Head word of the phrase
      - pets: Head word
- is: Linking Verb
- nice: Subject Complement and Adjective

### 3. The goal is for him to migrate the data
- The goal: Subject and NP
- is: Linking Verb
- for him to migrate the data: Non-Finite Infinitive Clause and Subject Complement
  - for: Complementizer
  - him: Overt agent
  - to migrate: Infinitive Transitive Verb
  - the data: DO and NP

### 4. The wheel of the car is big
- The wheel of the car: Subject and NP
  - The: Central determiner
  - wheel: Head word
  - of the car: Adjectival Post-Modifier and PP
- is: Linking Verb
- big: Subject Complement and Adjective

### 5. He agreed to attend
- He: Subject and Pronoun
- agreed: Intransitive Verb
- to attend: Verb Complement, Infinitive Phrase
  - He is the agent for the IP

### 6. True heroes are not afraid to make tough choices
- True heroes: Subject and NP
  - True: Adjective
  - heroes: Head word
- are not: Verb Phrase
  - are: Linking Verb
  - not: Sentential Negation Marker / Adverbial Specifier (Neg° / AdvP)
- afraid [complement]: Subject Complement and Adjective Phrase
  - afraid: Head word
  - to make [Phrase]: Complement of the Adjective and Infinitival Phrase
    - Agent is the subject of the sentence (True heroes)
    - to make: Head word and Monotransitive Verb
    - tough choices: DO and NP
      - tough: Adjective
      - choices: Head word
