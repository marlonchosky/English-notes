# Subject-Verb Agreement: Structural Head Primacy, Attraction Errors, and Agreement Domains

**Subject-Verb Agreement** (Formal Concord) requires a finite verb to copy the grammatical $\phi$-features (person and number) of the **Structural Head Noun ($N^\circ$)** within the Subject Determiner Phrase ($\text{DP}$), regardless of post-modifying phrases or intervening clauses.

---

## 1. The Core Rule: Structural Head Primacy

The finite verb agrees exclusively with the primary structural head noun of the subject phrase, completely ignoring nominals embedded inside post-modifying Prepositional Phrases ($\text{PPs}$), Relative Clauses ($\text{CPs}$), or participial phrases:

```text
       DP (Matrix Subject)
      /  \
    D°    NP
    |    /  \
   The  N'   PP (Adnominal Post-Modifier)
       /  \    \
      N°   ...  Object of the Preposition (Trapped Nominal)
    (Head)
      │
      └─────────────────── Controls Finite Verb Agreement (T°)
```

* *"The **quality** of these high-throughput database clusters **is** verified every sprint."*
  * **Head Noun ($N^\circ$):** *quality* (Singular) $\longrightarrow$ **Verb:** *is*
  * **Trapped Inside PP:** *database clusters* (Plural; ignored by syntax)
* *"The latest deployment **instructions** for the junior developer **were** committed to GitHub."*
  * **Head Noun ($N^\circ$):** *instructions* (Plural) $\longrightarrow$ **Verb:** *were*
  * **Trapped Inside PP:** *the junior developer* (Singular; ignored by syntax)
* *"The last **dog** of my parents' pets **is** nice."*
  * **Head Noun ($N^\circ$):** *dog* (Singular) $\longrightarrow$ **Verb:** *is*
* *"The last two **dogs** of my parents' pets **are** nice."*
  * **Head Noun ($N^\circ$):** *dogs* (Plural) $\longrightarrow$ **Verb:** *are*

---

## 2. The Proximity Trap (Attraction Errors)

An **Attraction Error** (or *Proximity Trap*) occurs when a finite verb mistakenly copies the number features of the linearly closest nominal (such as the object of an intervening preposition) rather than the structural head of the matrix subject:

* ❌ *\*"The **speed** of the network data transfers **have** increased significantly."*
  * **Root Cause:** Attraction error. The verb agrees with the local noun *transfers* instead of the singular structural head *speed*.
  * ✅ **Fix:** *"The **speed** of the network data transfers **has** increased significantly."*
* ❌ *\*"The **list** of registered microservice endpoints **are** corrupted."*
  * **Root Cause:** Attraction error caused by proximity to *endpoints*.
  * ✅ **Fix:** *"The **list** of registered microservice endpoints **is** corrupted."*
* ❌ *\*"The **cluster** of secondary storage nodes **reside** in availability zone B."*
  * **Root Cause:** Proximity error. *Cluster* is an opaque singular collective head noun.
  * ✅ **Fix:** *"The **cluster** of secondary storage nodes **resides** in availability zone B."*

## 3. Pronoun and Quantifier Head Primacy

When a Determiner Phrase lacks an overt lexical noun, the pronominal or quantifier head directly values the verb's number features:

### A. Cardinal Numbers ($N = 1$ vs. $N > 1$)
* **Cardinal Number $1$:** Enforces strict singular agreement as an opaque head:
  * *"**One** of the microservices **fails** during health checks."*
  * *"**One** of the container instances **was** terminated unexpectedly."*
* **Cardinal Numbers $> 1$:** Enforce plural agreement:
  * *"**Four** new worker threads **were** spawned by the orchestrator."*

### B. Distributive and Dual-Domain Operators
* **Pure Distributive (`Each`):** Operates as an opaque singular head ($N \ge 2$):
  * *"**Each** of the server logs **was** inspected by the engineer."*
* **Dual-Distributive (`Either`, `Neither`):** Strictly restricted to $N = 2$, enforcing singular agreement:
  * *"**Either** of the proposed solutions **is** acceptable to the architect."*
  * *"**Neither** of the two API keys **works** for authentication."*
* **Dual-Collective (`Both`):** Evaluates the pair together ($+2$), enforcing plural agreement:
  * *"**Both** of the endpoints **have** returned a 200 OK status."*

### C. Independent Definite and Possessive Pronouns
Independent pronouns inherit number features directly from their antecedents:
* *"My deployment script succeeded, but **yours are** still executing."* (Context: *yours* = *your deployment scripts* [Plural]).
* *"My config file is outdated, but **hers contains** the new credentials."* (Context: *hers* = *her config file* [Singular]).

## 4. Existential Inversion Constructions (*There*-Sentences)

In existential constructions introduced by expletive *there*, the syntactic subject position ($\text{Spec, TP}$) is occupied by a semantically empty placeholder. Formal concord is valued post-verbally by the **Notional Subject (The Pivot Nominal)**:

$$\mathbf{\text{Expletive \textit{There}}} + \mathbf{\text{Finite Verb}} + \mathbf{\text{[Pivot / Notional Subject DP]}}$$

* *"There **is** [DP an unhandled null pointer exception] in the payment handler."*
  * **Placeholder:** *There*
  * **Notional Subject:** *an unhandled null pointer exception* (Singular) $\longrightarrow$ **Verb:** *is*
* *"There **are** [DP three failing worker nodes] in the Kubernetes cluster."*
  * **Placeholder:** *There*
  * **Notional Subject:** *three failing worker nodes* (Plural) $\longrightarrow$ **Verb:** *are*

## 6. Syntactic Crash Diagnostics

### Diagnostic 1: Prepositional Attraction Error
* ❌ *\*"The **speed** of the network data transfers **have** increased significantly."*
* **Root Cause:** Proximity error. The speaker agrees with *transfers* rather than the singular head noun *speed*.
* ✅ **Fix:** *"The **speed** of the network data transfers **has** increased significantly."*

### Diagnostic 2: Cardinal Head Attraction Leak
* ❌ *\*"**One** of the database replicas **fail** health checks during peak load."*
* **Root Cause:** *One* is an opaque cardinal head noun with fixed $[+\text{Singular}]$ features. The plural complement inside the prepositional phrase (*replicas*) cannot transmit its number features to the matrix verb.
* ✅ **Fix:** *"**One** of the database replicas **fails** health checks during peak load."*

### Diagnostic 3: Distributive Dual Concord Leak
* ❌ *\*"**Neither** of the two load balancing algorithms **solve** the latency issue."*
* **Root Cause:** *Neither* is an opaque dual-distributive operator that evaluates items 1-by-1, strictly blocking plural concord.
* ✅ **Fix:** *"**Neither** of the two load balancing algorithms **solves** the latency issue."*

### Diagnostic 4: Existential Pivot Mismatch
* ❌ *\*"There **is** three failing worker nodes in the cluster."*
* **Root Cause:** In existential constructions, the finite verb agrees with the post-verbal pivot nominal (*three failing worker nodes*), which is plural.
* ✅ **Fix:** *"There **are** three failing worker nodes in the cluster."*
