# Adverbials of Measure and Duration

## 1. Conceptual Framework
Adverbials of Measure quantify magnitudes, extents, scale, or duration. Depending on whether they fulfill a required argument of the verb or add optional contextual detail, they act as **Complements** or **Adjuncts**.

---

## 2. Core Classifications & Subcategories

### A. Temporal Measure (Duration)
Answers **How long?** in terms of temporal extent.

* **Examples**: *for six hours*, *all day*, *briefly*, *throughout the night*.
* **Complement Example**: *"The migration lasted **for six hours**."*  
  *(The complex intransitive verb `last` requires a temporal measure argument; dropping it renders the clause incomplete).*
* **Adjunct Example**: *"We debugged the script **for six hours**."*  
  *(Optional context; dropping it leaves a complete sentence).*

---

### B. Spatial Measure (Distance / Extent)
Answers **How far?** across physical space or dimensions.

* **Examples**: *ten miles*, *a long way*, *three steps*.
* **Complement Example**: *"The pipeline extends **for ten miles**."*
* **Adjunct Example**: *"They walked **ten miles** yesterday."*

---

### C. Degree / Quantity / Extent
Answers **By how much?** or **To what extent?** regarding value, price, percentage, or intensity.

* **Examples**: *by ten percent*, *ten thousand dollars*, *significantly*, *to a great extent*.
* **Complement Example**: *"This server costs **ten thousand dollars**."*
* **Adjunct Example**: *"The index price rose **by ten percent**."*

---

## 3. Interrogative Realization: *How Much* vs. *How Many*

The syntax used to inquire about magnitudes depends on whether the underlying domain is continuous/mass or countable/discrete:

| Dimension           | Abstract Mass / Value (*How much*)         | Discrete Countable Units (*How many*)        |
| :------------------ | :----------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------- |
| **Monetary Value**  | *How **much** does this car cost?*         | *How **many** dollars/euros does it cost?*   |
| **Time / Duration** | *How **much** time did the build take?*    | *How **many** hours did the build take?*     |
| **Distance**        | *How **much** ground did we cover?*        | *How **many** miles/kilometers did we walk?* |
| **Weight / Mass**   | *How **much** does the server rack weigh?* | *How **many** kilograms does it weigh?*      |

> **Syntactic Rule:**
> - `How much` quantifies abstract continuous magnitudes directly (*cost*, *time*, *weight*).
> - `How many` must be paired with a plural countable noun serving as a discrete unit of measurement (*dollars*, *hours*, *kilometers*).
