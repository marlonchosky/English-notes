# Interrogative How-Phrases and Phrasal Containers

The interrogative word **`how`** is strictly a **Degree Adverb** ($Adv^\circ$) or **Manner Adverb** ($Adv^\circ$). It cannot function as a noun, pronoun, or determiner on its own. 

However, because degree adverbs license specifiers and modifiers across lexical and functional projections, **`how`** embeds directly inside larger phrases. When interrogative $Wh$-movement targets $\text{Spec, CP}$, it triggers **Pied-Piping of the Entire Container**, dragging the surrounding phrase along with it.

---

## 1. Core Syntactic Separation: Head vs. Phrasal Container

To accurately parse sentences introduced by *how*, syntax separates the **internal word class** of *how* from the **external structural container** that embeds it:

```text
The Phrasal Container Split
├── Internal Word Class:
│   "how" operates strictly as a Degree Operator (Adv°) modifying a quantifier, adverb, or adjective.
│
└── External Phrasal Container:
    The maximal projection (DP, AdvP, AP, or PP) that encapsulates "how"
    and lands in Spec, CP via Wh-movement.
```

---

## 2. The Primary Container Realizations

Depending on the syntactic category of the head constituent that *how* modifies, the fronted phrase projects as one of three primary structural containers:

### A. Determiner Phrase Containers ($\text{DP}$)

Occurs when *how* modifies a quantifier/determiner or an attributive adjective modifying an indefinite singular head noun.

#### 1. Quantifier Determiner Phrase Container
* **Internal Structure:** `[DP [AdvP How] + [D° much / many] + [NP Head Noun]]`
* **Target Modified:** The quantifier/determiner *much* (mass) or *many* (count).
* **Syntactic Functions:** Direct Object, Subject, or Measure Argument.
* **Examples:**
  * *"[DP **How much memory**]$_i$ does the worker node allocate $t_i$?"*  
    $\rightarrow$ **Container:** Determiner Phrase ($\text{DP}$). Function: Direct Object of *allocate*.  
    $\rightarrow$ **Word Classes:** `how` ($Adv^\circ$) $\rightarrow$ `much` ($D^\circ$) $\rightarrow$ `memory` ($N^\circ$).
  * *"[DP **How many microservices**]$_i$ crashed during deployment $t_i$?"*  
    $\rightarrow$ **Container:** Determiner Phrase ($\text{DP}$). Function: Subject of *crashed*.
  * *"[DP **How much ground**]$_i$ did the migration team cover $t_i$?"*  
    $\rightarrow$ **Container:** Determiner Phrase ($\text{DP}$). Function: Direct Object of *cover*.

#### 2. Inverted Degree Determiner Phrase Container (Pre-Article Inversion)
* **Internal Structure:** `[DP [AP How + Adjective] + [D° a / an] + [NP Head Noun]]`
* **Target Modified:** An attributive adjective modifying a singular indefinite noun.
* **Syntactic Functions:** Direct Object, Subject, or Predicate Complement.
* **Examples:**
  * *"[DP **How large a buffer size**]$_i$ do we need $t_i$?"*  
    $\rightarrow$ **Container:** Determiner Phrase ($\text{DP}$). Function: Direct Object of *need*.  
    $\rightarrow$ **Syntactic Mechanism:** The degree operator *how* modifies *large*, forcing the $[AdvP + AP]$ complex to invert to the left of the central determiner *a/an*.
  * *"[DP **How critical a vulnerability**]$_i$ did the audit identify $t_i$?"*  
    $\rightarrow$ **Container:** Determiner Phrase ($\text{DP}$). Function: Direct Object of *identify*.

---

### B. Adverb Phrase Containers ($\text{AdvP}$)

Occurs when *how* modifies another adverb of manner, frequency, or degree without a following head noun.

* **Internal Structure:** `[AdvP [Adv° How] + [Adv° Head Adverb]]`
* **Target Modified:** A manner, frequency, or degree adverb head.
* **Syntactic Function:** Adverbial Adjunct modifying the matrix verb phrase.
* **Examples:**
  * *"[AdvP **How fast**]$_i$ did the benchmark script execute $t_i$?"*  
    $\rightarrow$ **Container:** Adverb Phrase ($\text{AdvP}$). Function: Manner Adverbial Adjunct modifying *execute*.
  * *"[AdvP **How often**]$_i$ do health check probes ping the ingress gateway $t_i$?"*  
    $\rightarrow$ **Container:** Adverb Phrase ($\text{AdvP}$). Function: Frequency Adverbial Adjunct modifying *ping*.
  * *"[AdvP **How much**]$_i$ do the stakeholders value this performance fix $t_i$?"*  
    $\rightarrow$ **Container:** Adverb Phrase ($\text{AdvP}$). Function: Degree Adverbial Adjunct modifying *value*. *(No head noun present).*

---

### C. Adjective Phrase Containers ($\text{AP}$)

Occurs when *how* modifies a predicative scalar adjective.

* **Internal Structure:** `[AP [Adv° How] + [A° Head Adjective]]`
* **Target Modified:** A predicate, scalar, or evaluative adjective.
* **Syntactic Function:** Subject Complement ($\text{SC}$) completing a copular verb.
* **Examples:**
  * *"[AP **How resilient**]$_i$ is the distributed consensus protocol $t_i$?"*  
    $\rightarrow$ **Container:** Adjective Phrase ($\text{AP}$). Function: Subject Complement of copula *is*.
  * *"[AP **How tall**]$_i$ is the server rack enclosure $t_i$?"*  
    $\rightarrow$ **Container:** Adjective Phrase ($\text{AP}$). Function: Subject Complement of copula *is*.
  * *"[AP **How stable**]$_i$ did the cluster remain during failover $t_i$?"*  
    $\rightarrow$ **Container:** Adjective Phrase ($\text{AP}$). Function: Subject Complement of aspectual copula *remain*.

## 4. The Pied-Piping Constraint (Left-Branch Condition)

Under generative syntax, the Left Branch Condition and phrasal island constraints prevent *how* from extracting independently out of its host phrase:

* ✅ **Valid (Pied-Piping of Entire Container):**
  * $\text{[DP How much memory]}_i \text{ did you reserve } t_i\text{?}$
  * $\text{[AdvP How fast]}_i \text{ did the daemon process the queue } t_i\text{?}$
  * $\text{[AP How resilient]}_i \text{ was the failover node } t_i\text{?}$
* ❌ **Syntactic Crash (Left-Branch Extraction / Stranded Head):**
  * *\* $\text{How}_i \text{ did you reserve [DP } t_i \text{ much memory]?}$*
  * *\* $\text{How}_i \text{ did the daemon process the queue [AdvP } t_i \text{ fast]?}$*
  * *\* $\text{How}_i \text{ was the failover node [AP } t_i \text{ resilient]?}$*

## 5. Complex Prepositional Pied-Piping vs. Stranding

When a $How$-phrase functions as the Object of a Preposition inside a $\text{PP}$, syntax licenses two transformation strategies:

```text
Prepositional Transformations
├── A. Preposition Stranding (Informal / Standard Register):
│      Only the DP container extracts to Spec, CP; the preposition P° remains stranded.
│      • "[How large a database] are you connecting [to t_i]?"
│      • "[How high a throughput] did the cluster benchmark [at t_i]?"
│
└── B. Prepositional Pied-Piping (High Formal / Academic Register):
       The preposition P° hitches a ride with the entire DP container to Spec, CP.
       • "[PP To how large a database] are you connecting t_i?"
       • "[PP At how high a throughput] did the cluster benchmark t_i?"
```

## 6. Structural Guardrails & Crash Rules

### Guardrail 1: The Pre-Article Word Order Constraint
When *how* modifies an attributive adjective modifying a singular count noun, the degree phrase **must** precede the central article ($a/an$). Trapping *how* behind the indefinite article triggers an immediate crash:
* ❌ *\*A how scalable infrastructure can the team deploy?*
* ✅ *[DP [AP How scalable] a system] can the team deploy?*
* ❌ *\*The team observed a how critical memory leak.*
* ✅ *The team observed [DP [AP how critical] a memory leak] it was.*

### Guardrail 2: The Functional Role Split (*Direct Object* vs. *Measure Adjunct*)
Do not confuse the phrasal container type ($\text{DP}$) with its matrix grammatical function:
* **Direct Object ($\text{DP}$):** Selected by a monotransitive verb:
  * *"[DP How much ground]$_i$ did the crawler cover $t_i$?"* (Passivization: *"Ground was covered"* $\longrightarrow$ **Direct Object**).
* **Spatial/Temporal Measure Adjunct ($\text{DP}$):** Attaches optionally to an intransitive motion or duration verb:
  * *"[DP How many kilometers]$_i$ did the drone travel $t_i$?"* (*travel* is intransitive; phrase acts as an optional **Spatial Measure Adjunct**).
