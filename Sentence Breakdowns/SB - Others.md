1. He bought a car for his brother
   - bought: Monotransitive Verb
   - a car: DO and NP
   - for his brother: Prepositional Adjunct (a type of Verb Adjunct) and PP
2. He turned the page.
   - turned: Monotransitive Verb
   - the page: DO and NP
3. The car is making strange noises
   - The car: Subject and NP
   - is: Auxiliary
   - making: Monotransitive
   - strange noises: DO and NP
     - strange: Attributive Adjective
     - noises: head word
5. I have something to sit next to
   - something: DO and NP
   - to sit next to: Adjectival Post-Modifier, and an Infinitive Phrase
     - to sit: head word of the IP. Its agent is "I", and it is intransitive
       - Logic: I have something [for **me**] to sit next to
     - next to: Complex Preposition and Stranded Preposition
6. The goal is that he migrate the data
   - is: CIV
   - that he migrate the data: Subject Complement and Subjunctive Noun Clause 
   - that: Mandative Subordinator for Subjunctive
   - migrate: Bare Infinitive Monotransitive Verb in Subjunctive
7. Him leaving the company was sad.
   - Him leaving the company: Subject and Gerund Phrase
     - Him: Agent and pronoun in the object case
     - leaving: head word of the GP, monotransitive in this case
     - the company: DO and NP, Object of "leaving"
   - was: CIV
   - sad: Subject Complement and Adjective
8. I agreed that our team is better.
   - agreed: Intransitive Verb
   - that our team is better: Noun Clause
     - that: Complementizer
     - our team: Subject and NP
     - is: CIV
     - better: Subject Complement and Comparative Adjective
9. The paper which was written last night is ok
   - The paper [phrase]: Subject and NP
     - [phrase] = which was written last night: Relative Clause and Adjective Clause
       - which: Subject and Relative Pronoun
       - was written: Passive Verb Phrase
       - was: Auxiliary Verb for the Passive Voice
       - written: Main Verb in the Passive Voice
       - last night: Temporal Adverbial Adjunct and NP
   - is: CIV
   - ok: Subject Complement and Adjective
